# How to contribute

BibleGet is an open project, and much of its work is careful checking: of the text, of the links between editions, of
the pages you are reading. There are several ways to help.

## Reviewing the alignment of editions

The links from each edition's verses to the [textual units](/docs/textual-units) of CBTUDR are made by rule where a
rule decides, and drafted by tools where it does not. Drafts are published in the registry marked as drafts, and each is
checked by a person, whose review marks it reviewed. Reviewers work on this site, at `/review`: chapter by chapter, starting with the drafts the tools were least
sure of, they see each verse beside the units it may be linked to, and accept, correct or relink it. The reviewed links
then go to the registry as contributions to [CatholicOS/cbtudr](https://github.com/CatholicOS/cbtudr), where who
reviewed what is kept in the history.

Reviewing needs a good reading knowledge of the edition's language (the units are based on the Latin of the Nova
Vulgata, so some Latin helps) and a reviewer's role on your account: if you would like to help, open an issue in the
[CBTUDR repository](https://github.com/CatholicOS/cbtudr/issues).

## Reporting an error in the text

If a verse on this site reads differently from the printed edition, a word is missing, a line is broken in the wrong
place, or a heading is out of place, please report it in the [endpoint's
issues](https://github.com/BibleGet-I-O/endpoint/issues), giving the edition, the reference, what the site shows and
what the printed edition has. Errors are corrected against the edition's own source; where the source itself is wrong,
the error is reported to its publisher.

A misprint in an edition is not corrected silently. Where a note or an introduction cites a wrong verse, the citation
stays as printed and the site adds, in red, what was meant: *[sic! expected …]*.

## Reporting a problem with the site

A link that leads nowhere, a page that does not work on your device, a mistake in these documentation pages, or a
translation that reads badly: please open an issue in the [website's
repository](https://github.com/BibleGet-I-O/website/issues). These pages are written in English first and then
translated; readers who can check a translation against the English are especially welcome.
