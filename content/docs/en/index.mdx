# Documentation

The Bible on this site comes in several editions and languages, and they do not always agree: a verse has another
number, a book is longer, a psalm is counted differently. These pages tell how the text of the Bible reached us, from the
Hebrew scriptures and their Greek translation, through the great manuscripts and Jerome's Latin, to the editions you can
read here, and so why they differ.

The first two pages are for every reader: [how the Bible reached us](/docs/how-the-bible-reached-us), in one page, and
[why editions differ](/docs/why-editions-differ), which the reader's small "why?" marks open. The pages on manuscripts
and traditions go further, for students: each tells what a manuscript or a tradition is, where it is kept, and what it
means for the text you read. Pages on each edition on this site, and on the project's work on identifying the same
passage across editions, will follow.
